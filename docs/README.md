# OctoAcme Project Management Docs

OctoAcme manages cross-functional projects through a lightweight lifecycle: initiate and validate the work, plan scope and delivery, execute in small increments, release and verify changes, and capture learnings through retrospectives and continuous improvement. Clear ownership, stakeholder communication, and risk management support every stage.

## Project lifecycle and guidance

1. [Project Management Overview](octoacme-project-management-overview.md) — principles, roles, key artifacts, and the end-to-end lifecycle.
2. [Project Initiation Guide](octoacme-project-initiation.md) — validate the opportunity, align stakeholders, and decide whether to proceed to planning.
3. [Project Planning](octoacme-project-planning.md) — turn approved work into a prioritized backlog, delivery plan, and risk register.
4. [Execution & Tracking](octoacme-execution-and-tracking.md) — coordinate day-to-day delivery, quality practices, progress tracking, and blocker escalation.
5. [Release & Deployment Guide](octoacme-release-and-deployment.md) — prepare, deploy, verify, communicate, and roll back releases when needed.
6. [Retrospective & Continuous Improvement](octoacme-retrospective-and-continuous-improvement.md) — capture learnings and track focused improvement actions.

## Practices across the lifecycle

- [Risk Management & Communication](octoacme-risks-and-communication.md) — identify and monitor risks and dependencies, keep stakeholders informed, and follow escalation paths.
- [Roles & Personas](octoacme-roles-and-personas.md) — understand the responsibilities and communication needs of developers, product managers, and project managers.

Use these guides together with the project’s charter or one-pager, backlog, risk register, and release plan. Keep project-specific status and decisions in the project’s agreed single source of truth, and adapt meeting cadence and delivery practices to the team’s needs.
