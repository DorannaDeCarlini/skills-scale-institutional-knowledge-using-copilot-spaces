# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

### Interactions with Other Roles
- Collaborate with Product Managers to clarify outcomes and with Project Managers to surface estimates, dependencies, and delivery risks.
- Work with QA/Testing on testability and acceptance, and with Technical Leads or Architects on design constraints.

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

### Interactions with Other Roles
- Align with Project Managers on timing and feasibility and with UX/Design on customer needs.
- Partner with Business Analysis, Data/Analytics, Developers, and QA/Testing to translate outcomes into clear, measurable work.

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

### Interactions with Other Roles
- Coordinate plans and decisions with Product Managers, Delivery Facilitators, Developers, QA/Testing, and Stakeholders.
- Work with Technical Leads on technical dependencies and Release Managers on release readiness, schedule, and risk.

---

## Release Manager

### Role Summary
Release Managers coordinate the planning and execution of production releases, ensuring readiness and controlled deployment.

### Responsibilities
- Plan release windows and coordinate readiness checks
- Confirm acceptance criteria, CI status, security checks, and rollback plans
- Coordinate staging and production deployments
- Manage release communications and post-deployment verification
- Capture release lessons and coordinate follow-up actions

### Goals
- Deliver releases safely and predictably
- Minimize deployment risk and downtime
- Improve release visibility and operational readiness

### Typical Communication
- Release readiness reviews and go/no-go discussions
- Deployment plans and status updates
- Incident and rollback coordination

### Interactions with Other Roles
- Work with Project Managers on release schedules and risks, Developers and QA/Testing on readiness, and Technical Leads on technical constraints.
- Coordinate announcements and support preparation with Stakeholders and Customer Support/Success.

---

## Technical Lead / Architect

### Role Summary
Technical Leads and Architects set technical direction, guide implementation choices, and identify architectural constraints and risks.

### Responsibilities
- Establish technical direction and development standards
- Review design trade-offs and integration dependencies
- Identify technical risks and propose mitigations
- Guide implementation and resolve cross-team technical issues
- Ensure maintainability, reliability, and observability requirements are addressed

### Goals
- Provide coherent, sustainable technical solutions
- Reduce integration and operational risk
- Improve technical quality and shared engineering practices

### Typical Communication
- Technical design reviews and architecture discussions
- Planning reviews and cross-team dependency updates
- Incident and risk reviews

### Interactions with Other Roles
- Guide Developers on implementation and collaborate with QA/Testing on testability and quality risks.
- Align with Product Managers on feasibility and trade-offs, Project Managers on dependencies, and Security/Compliance on controls.

---

## UX / Design Lead

### Role Summary
UX and Design Leads ensure that solutions are usable, accessible, and grounded in customer needs and validated product expectations.

### Responsibilities
- Define interaction patterns and usability goals
- Conduct or synthesize customer research
- Produce and review designs, prototypes, and accessibility guidance
- Define UX acceptance criteria and validate implementation
- Incorporate feedback and usage insights into improvements

### Goals
- Improve usability and accessibility
- Align design decisions with customer and business outcomes
- Reduce costly rework caused by unclear interaction requirements

### Typical Communication
- Design reviews and usability sessions
- Product discovery and acceptance reviews
- Feedback and implementation coordination

### Interactions with Other Roles
- Partner with Product Managers on customer outcomes and priorities and with Business Analysis on workflow and requirement clarity.
- Collaborate with Developers on implementation, QA/Testing on usability acceptance, and Stakeholders on needs and feedback.

---

## Scrum Master / Delivery Facilitator

### Role Summary
Scrum Masters or Delivery Facilitators support a healthy delivery process, facilitate ceremonies, and help the team remove impediments.

### Responsibilities
- Facilitate agreed planning, review, and retrospective ceremonies
- Help the team apply and improve delivery practices
- Identify and escalate delivery impediments
- Promote psychological safety and continuous improvement
- Support predictable team rhythms and focus

### Goals
- Improve delivery flow and team effectiveness
- Surface impediments early
- Strengthen feedback and continuous improvement

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and planning sessions
- Retrospectives and action-item follow-up
- Delivery impediment escalations

### Interactions with Other Roles
- Work with Project Managers on cross-team dependencies and risks and with Product Managers on backlog readiness and team flow.
- Facilitate collaboration between Developers, QA/Testing, UX/Design, and other delivery participants.

---

## Business Analyst / Requirements Lead

### Role Summary
Business Analysts or Requirements Leads clarify needs, document workflows, and ensure requirements remain traceable to outcomes and acceptance criteria.

### Responsibilities
- Elicit and clarify stakeholder and customer requirements
- Map current and target workflows
- Document business rules and dependencies
- Maintain traceability between requirements, success metrics, and acceptance criteria
- Facilitate requirement and scope clarification

### Goals
- Reduce ambiguity and rework
- Improve requirement quality and traceability
- Keep implementation aligned with business and customer needs

### Typical Communication
- Requirement workshops and reviews
- Stakeholder interviews and process documentation
- Backlog refinement and acceptance discussions

### Interactions with Other Roles
- Partner with Product Managers on outcomes and priorities and with Stakeholders on requirements and validation.
- Provide clarity to Developers and UX/Design and help QA/Testing turn requirements into test coverage.

---

## Data / Analytics Lead

### Role Summary
Data and Analytics Leads define measurement approaches and interpret evidence to guide product and project decisions.

### Responsibilities
- Define measurement and instrumentation plans
- Validate success metrics and reporting methods
- Analyze adoption, quality, and performance signals
- Communicate findings and limitations clearly
- Support evidence-based prioritization and retrospectives

### Goals
- Improve data-informed decision making
- Ensure success metrics are measurable and trustworthy
- Identify trends and opportunities for improvement

### Typical Communication
- Metrics reviews and dashboard updates
- Experiment and research analysis
- Stakeholder outcome reports

### Interactions with Other Roles
- Work with Product Managers to define and interpret outcomes and with Developers and Technical Leads to ensure reliable instrumentation.
- Share findings with Project Managers, QA/Testing, UX/Design, and Stakeholders for planning and validation.

---

## Customer Support / Success Representative

### Role Summary
Customer Support and Success Representatives represent customer experience, operational readiness, and field feedback throughout delivery.

### Responsibilities
- Provide customer and support feedback to the delivery team
- Define support readiness and escalation needs
- Identify known issues and recurring customer pain points
- Prepare support materials and communicate operational changes
- Share post-release customer impact and feedback

### Goals
- Improve customer experience and support readiness
- Reduce support surprises from releases
- Surface real-world issues early

### Typical Communication
- Release readiness and support-readiness reviews
- Customer feedback sessions and incident updates
- Support documentation and stakeholder communications

### Interactions with Other Roles
- Work with Product Managers and UX/Design on customer needs and with Project Managers and Release Managers on operational impact.
- Connect Developers and QA/Testing to customer scenarios and bring Stakeholders timely customer feedback.

---

## Security / Compliance Representative

### Role Summary
Security and Compliance Representatives advise on controls, privacy, security obligations, and regulatory or policy requirements.

### Responsibilities
- Identify applicable security and compliance requirements
- Review threats, risks, and control gaps
- Advise on secure design and testing expectations
- Coordinate vulnerability and security incident escalation
- Validate evidence and readiness for relevant releases

### Goals
- Reduce security and compliance risk
- Embed controls into normal delivery practices
- Provide clear, timely escalation and guidance

### Typical Communication
- Security and compliance reviews
- Threat and risk assessments
- Incident, vulnerability, and audit coordination

### Interactions with Other Roles
- Advise Technical Leads and Developers on secure implementation and QA/Testing on security validation.
- Coordinate risk and release concerns with Project Managers and Release Managers and communicate expectations to Product Managers and Stakeholders.

---

## How these personas are used in the exercise
- Use these persona definitions to frame scenarios and sample interactions in the Skills Exercise.
- Each persona can be used as a persona prompt for Copilot Spaces to shape role-specific guidance.
- Apply roles selectively based on project needs while preserving clear ownership, avoiding duplicated accountability, and documenting decision and escalation paths.
